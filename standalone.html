Questions

When should we commission Larish Events?

We accept a limited number of twelve full-scale productions each year. We recommend inquiring 9 to 18 months prior to your desired celebration date to secure iconic historic estates.

Do you orchestrate multi-day destination celebrations?

Yes. We specialize in comprehensive weekend wedding experiences including welcome aperitivos, scenic excursions, main gala evenings, and restorative recovery brunches.

Which destinations do you specialize in?

Our primary hubs are Rome, Florence, Lake Como, the Amalfi Coast, Venice, and the French Riviera, along with select private estates worldwide.

How is the creative and floral direction handled?

Every visual element is designed in-house through 3D spatial renders, material samples, and direct collaborations with Europe's finest floral artisans.

Can you accommodate private estates or non-traditional venues?

Absolutely. Our production engineers build bespoke temporary infrastructure—including luxury climate-controlled pavilions, power grids, and commercial satellite kitchens.
